### Scientific/model reviewer — unassigned

1. Review the selected activity separately from the curriculum mapping, and record exact model/version, assumptions, limitations and examples.
2. Decide whether the proposed bulb or LED representation is pedagogically appropriate for the selected Outcome. If an LED model is used, verify orientation, forward-voltage approximation, resistor assumptions and allowed voltage/current ranges.
3. Review open/closed path, short or invalid topology, branch output, units, thresholds and golden cases. Distinguish normal simulated states from behavior outside the scientific model.
4. Review age-appropriate low-voltage safety language; the virtual model must not imply that students should experiment on household mains.
